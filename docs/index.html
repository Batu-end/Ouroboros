Ouroboros

Stop when your focus breaks.
Get the time back.

No 25-minute blocks. Work for as long as it lasts, then press one button. Ouroboros turns 15% of the time you actually worked into a break.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

macOS 14+ · v0.1.0 · no account, no network

What it does

Small on purpose.

01

Breaks are earned

An hour of work buys nine minutes off. Even two minutes buys one. There is never a reason to push through.

02

Quitting is the main button

The biggest control says “I'm Distracted — Take Break.” Stopping early isn't failing. It's how the loop works.

03

Lives in the menu bar

No Dock icon, no window to lose. Colour tells you the phase: work, rest, or waiting for you to come back.

04

Quiet and private

0% CPU at idle, no dependencies, no network code. Your history stays on your Mac for 60 days, then it's gone.

How it works

One loop.

  1. 01

    Start working

    Click the icon. The timer counts up, with no target to hit.

  2. 02

    Stop when it breaks

    Press the button. 15% of what you worked becomes your break.

  3. 03

    Come back

    A soft chime when it's over. It waits, counting nothing, until you click.

Beta

This is an early release. It's tested on one machine (Apple silicon, macOS 26), and the Intel build has never been run. Expect rough edges.

It isn't notarized yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Bug reports help most right now, especially from other macOS versions and Intel Macs.
